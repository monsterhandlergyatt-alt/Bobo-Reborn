- Chapter title

## Style notes
- Balance jungle life, friendship, cars, and chaotic comedy.
- Let emotional moments breathe, especially around the Del Sol.
- Keep character voices distinct.
- Twixxel's Vit Style should be recognizable but readable.
- Avoid making the story nothing but constant cursing or unrelated escalation.

## Canon status
A funny one-off gag does not automatically become permanent canon. Update [../CANON.md](../CANON.md) when a detail is confirmed.
