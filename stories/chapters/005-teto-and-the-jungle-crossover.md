## Teto's surprise deliveries

A fan-requested running gag became part of the jungle crew's routine: **Teto keeps coming back with surprises for Twixxel, and sometimes she brings one for ThatMob too.**

One day, Twixxel was minding his own business when Teto returned with something for him.

“Twixxel! I brought you something!”

Twixxel looked up immediately. “Vait—really? For me? Vat is it?”

Teto held the surprise out of sight for one extra second, clearly enjoying the suspense.

“Guess!”

Twixxel tried to peek around her. “Is it food? A tiny creature? Something zat makes a suspicious noise?”

“Why are those your first guesses?” ThatMob asked.

“Because zis is Teto,” Twixxel replied, as if that explained absolutely everything.

Teto finally presented the surprise. Twixxel's eyes lit up—whatever it was, she had clearly picked it out especially for him. ThatMob leaned closer, hoping Teto might have brought something for him too.

“Maybe next time,” Teto teased.

“Zey have a next time already?!” Twixxel blurted out.

Teto just grinned. Around here, a visit from her could mean a thoughtful little gift, something completely bizarre, or the beginning of a brand-new jungle disaster. The exact surprise can vary from chapter to chapter; the important part is that Teto keeps returning with them.

The musical scenes work best when they grow out of the characters' reactions: Teto getting annoyed, someone pushing the joke too far, a song becoming the center of attention, or Teto unexpectedly arriving with another gift. Even with the crossover cast, the jungle remains the grounding point.
