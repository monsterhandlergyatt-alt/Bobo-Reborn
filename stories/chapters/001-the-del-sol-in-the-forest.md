# Chapter 001 — The Del Sol in the Forest

> Archive retelling reconstructed from remembered story beats; not a verbatim transcript.

Bobo woke in the forest with the jungle quiet around him. Between the trees sat a familiar shape beneath layers of rust: his 1995 Honda Civic Del Sol.

The car was abandoned, rusty, and would not start. Bobo stood still, recognizing something he had thought was gone. This was not just a wreck. It belonged to a part of his life he had missed.

He opened the glovebox. Inside was the key.

“I thought you were gone,” he whispered.

He couldn't undo the years the car had spent outside, and he couldn't make it run by wishing. But he could refuse to leave it there.

“I'm not leaving you here. You're gonna run again.”

Twixxel_lesgo and ThatMob became part of the situation as Bobo faced the work ahead. The car needed to be moved, inspected, and restored. It would take time, rocks, and patience.

Bobo looked at the Del Sol one more time. He had found his Del Sol. Now he had to bring it home.

**Continuity:** The car is a 1995 Honda Civic Del Sol, has no rear seats, is rusty and non-running when found, and has its key in the glovebox.
