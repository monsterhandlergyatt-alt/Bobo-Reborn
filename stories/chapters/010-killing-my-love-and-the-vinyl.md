# Chapter 010 — Killing My Love

> Archive retelling based on established emotional beats; not a verbatim transcript.

Eurobeat had become part of Bobo's car world, but it was not always just background music. Some songs carried feelings that Bobo did not easily explain out loud.

“Killing My Love” was one of those songs.

Lila had found a vinyl record of “Killing My Love,” and the discovery hit Bobo hard. The song could make him cry because it was tied to memories and emotions that ran deeper than the beat. The moment was a reminder that the loud, chaotic car-loving monkey had a softer side.

The scene did not need to turn into a joke immediately. Bobo could have a quiet moment with the record and let the feeling land. His care for music also extended to the car itself: he liked his speakers, but he did not want to destroy them by blasting everything at maximum volume.
