# Chapter 011 — The Eurobeat Comeback

> Archive retelling of the remembered music thread; exact original dialogue is not preserved.

For a while, the story had a “no more Euro” turn. Eurobeat was pushed aside, as if the jungle could simply move on from one of Bobo's defining interests.

But it did not stay gone.

Eurobeat was brought back into the story, because it belonged to the saga's car culture and to Bobo's emotional connection with driving. It could fuel an Initial D-style moment, make a garage scene feel triumphant, or bring back the memory attached to “Killing My Love.”

The comeback worked best as a return of something familiar rather than a replacement for every other kind of music or scene. Bobo could enjoy the soundtrack without having to crank the volume to its absolute maximum.

**Continuity:** Eurobeat is important; there was a “no more Eurobeat” phase followed by its return. “Killing My Love” retains its special emotional meaning.
