# Chapter 003 — The Del Sol Restoration

> Consolidated archive retelling of the known restoration arc, not a verbatim transcript.

The Del Sol sat in the garage looking like a promise that would take real work to keep.

Bobo and Koda started with the basics. A car that had spent years in the forest could not be fixed with a quick polish and a dramatic turn of the key. Problems had to be diagnosed; rusty areas, worn parts, and mechanical issues needed attention.

The parts list grew. Bobo bought expensive pieces for the project, sometimes staring at the cost as if the numbers might change. Rocks were the currency, but the pile seemed to shrink faster than he wanted. Still, he kept going. He wanted a proper restoration, not a half-finished repair.

The garage filled with the rhythm of a long project: old problems being uncovered, parts arriving, work taking longer than expected, and Bobo returning to the car because stopping halfway was not an option. Koda stayed alongside him through the project.

Eventually, the restoration was complete. The Del Sol was no longer the dead, rusty car Bobo had found among the trees. Bobo had kept his promise.

**Continuity:** Discovery, transport, diagnosis, expensive parts and repairs, then completion. Specific paint and repair details remain open unless separately confirmed.
