# Chapter 002 — Bringing the Del Sol Home

> Archive retelling of the established milestones; exact original wording may differ.

Finding the Del Sol was the emotional part. Getting it out of the forest was the practical part. Bobo had found the car he thought was lost, but it couldn't stay where it was.

The tree was a familiar stop along the way—a place to pause and take in what finding the car meant. The Del Sol looked rough in the open, but Bobo could imagine it being more than a rusting shell.

The next destination was home, where Bobo lived with Koda. Koda could see the car mattered to Bobo. This was not just another project to fill the garage; Bobo wanted to give a piece of his past another chance.

Once the car reached the garage, the real work could begin. It needed inspection and diagnosis, not a magical instant fix. Rust, mechanical condition, tires, fluids, wiring, and the unknown effects of years abandoned all had to be considered.

The parts list was going to grow. The project would cost rocks and test Bobo's patience, but the Del Sol was home.

**Continuity:** The emotional route is forest → tree → home/garage. Restoration happens in stages.
