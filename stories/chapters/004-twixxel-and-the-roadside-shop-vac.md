# Chapter 004 — Twixxel and the Roadside Shop-Vac

> Reconstructed compilation of the remembered Popper/shop-vac rounds; not a verbatim transcript.

Twixxel found a shop-vac beside the road and decided that bringing it home was a perfectly reasonable idea. The machine became known as **Popper**.

At first it seemed like a lucky find. Then the warning signs started. The shop-vac made suspicious noises and turned a simple cleaning plan into a looming disaster. Twixxel, rather than developing a sensible safety plan, found the situation exciting. He danced, delighted in the chaos, and acted as if a potentially explosive machine was the best entertainment the jungle had ever provided.

There was one important complication: Bibi must not find out.

Popper's adventures became a cycle of suspicious cleaning, alarming signs, a sudden bang or pop, and frantic attempts to hide the evidence. Twixxel tried to conceal or return the machine without Bibi discovering what happened. Each round escalated.

By **Round Six**, Bobo was getting dangerously close to finding out. Twixxel had to keep up his innocent act while the evidence made that harder.

Later, another roadside shop-vac appeared—more scratched up and even more suspicious than Popper. At first it seemed switched off. Twixxel kicked it, then flipped the switch, setting up another potential disaster.

The lesson was apparently not to stop picking up suspicious machines from the roadside. The lesson was that the next one might be even more exciting.

**Voice note:** Twixxel's voice is soft and cute. Use occasional readable “Vit Style” words—*ze, zat, zis, vit, zey, vere, vat, tink, ting, zen,* and *zere*—without forcing them into every sentence.

**Continuity:** Popper is the original shop-vac; Bibi finding out is recurring tension; Round Six nearly exposes Twixxel to Bobo; a second, more scratched-up shop-vac appears later.
