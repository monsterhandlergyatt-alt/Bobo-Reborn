# Bobo Reborn — Location Guide

The jungle is the heart of this era. Recurring places should feel familiar and gain meaning through repeated visits.

## The jungle
The main setting for everyday life, discoveries, travel, and unexpected chaos. Keep it lush, lived-in, and connected to the characters' home.

## Bobo and Koda's home
Their shared home base for quiet conversations, planning, arguments, jokes, and the aftermath of garage disasters. The exact floor plan is open unless a scene establishes it.

## The tree
A familiar landmark in the Del Sol rediscovery arc. Bringing the car to the tree is an important emotional step between finding it abandoned and bringing it home.

## The garage
The center of the Del Sol restoration. Repairs should have stages: inspection, diagnosis, sourcing parts, fixing problems, testing, and finally enjoying the finished car. Progress should feel earned.

## The forest discovery site
Where Bobo finds his rusty, non-running 1995 Honda Civic Del Sol. The key is in the glovebox. The discovery should feel personal, not like finding an ordinary abandoned vehicle.

## Adding locations
Add a place here when it becomes recurring or important. Keep new places connected to jungle life whenever practical, and mark new details as provisional until confirmed.
