# Running Gags, Props, and Motifs

This is a continuity aid, not a rule that every story must use every gag. Reuse familiar elements when they fit.

## Rocks as money
Rocks are the saga's currency. Use them naturally in purchases, bargains, and jokes.

## Garage chaos
Careful restoration plans can collide with impulsive decisions, confusing tools, expensive parts, and spectacularly bad ideas. Let chaos escalate while keeping the Del Sol's restoration milestones coherent.

## Popper and the roadside shop-vac incidents
Twixxel's shop-vac misadventures are recurring comedy material. Warning signs, suspicious noises, dancing, explosions, and attempts to hide the evidence can escalate. Each repeat should add a new twist rather than copy the last one.

## Teto's surprise deliveries
This recurring gag began as a fan request: whenever Teto comes back from somewhere, she brings a surprise for Twixxel. Sometimes she brings a surprise for ThatMob too, but he's the occasional recipient rather than the main target. The contents can range from sweet and harmless to bafflingly chaotic; keep the surprise itself fresh, and let Twixxel's reaction sell the joke. ThatMob may think he's safe until Teto reveals she brought him something as well. This is a running gag, not a requirement that every Teto entrance must include a gift.

## Eurobeat and “Killing My Love”
Eurobeat is part of Bobo's car culture. “Killing My Love” has special emotional meaning for him. Music can make a scene funny or sincere, but Bobo doesn't simply blast his speakers at maximum volume all the time.

## Cars are personal
Bobo's Del Sol is not just transportation: its rediscovery and restoration mean something to him. Respect the real layout of the 1995 Honda Civic Del Sol—there are no rear seats.

## Continuity guardrails
- Keep established relationships consistent.
- Don't overuse a gag until it stops being funny.
- Leave room for quiet scenes between chaotic ones.
- Add permanent props or gags here when confirmed; update canon only when a detail is truly established.
