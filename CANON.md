# Bobo Saga Canon

- The saga began around late 2024 or early 2025; the current era is **Bobo Reborn**.
- The jungle is the central setting. Rocks are the currency.
- **Bobo** is the main character; Bobo, not Popo.
- **Koda** is Bobo's companion/brother, lives with him in the jungle, and loves Bobo.
- **Lila** is Bobo's girlfriend and has a Del Sol.
- **Aaron** is a fun-loving monkey; manual/stick shift is a driving complication.
- **Mochi** is a cat hacker from the older heist crew, predating Bobo.
- **Twixxel_lesgo** and **ThatMob** are part of the Bobo Reborn cast.
- **Eisuke** is a normal human, not Eisuke_Fur or a fursuit character.
- **SlipperyHC** should not be labeled a furry; distinguish Minecraft skin from real-world identity.
- Bobo's signature car is a **1995 Honda Civic Del Sol**, with no rear seats. It is found rusty and non-running in the forest, key in glovebox, and later restored in the garage.
- Koda initially has no car; Bobo later gives him a Toyota/AE86-related car.
- Eurobeat matters; “Killing My Love” is emotionally significant to Bobo. He avoids blasting speakers at maximum volume.
- Twixxel has a soft, cute voice and occasional readable German-inspired “Vit Style” (ze, zat, zis, vit, zey, vere, vat, tink, ting, zen, zere). Don't force it into every sentence.
- Don't make the cast curse constantly. Comedy and sincere moments can coexist.
- Preserve established relationships and mark unknown details as open.
