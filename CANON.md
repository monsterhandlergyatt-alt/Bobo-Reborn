# Bobo Saga Canon

## Era and setting

- The Bobo Saga began around late 2024 or early 2025.
- The current era is called **Bobo Reborn**.
- The jungle is the core setting. Keep new locations focused and purposeful.
- **Rocks** are the saga's currency.

## Core relationships

- **Bobo** is the central character.
- **Koda** is Bobo's companion/brother and lives with him in the jungle. Koda loves Bobo.
- **Lila** is Bobo's girlfriend. She also has a Del Sol.
- **Mochi** is a cat and hacker associated with the older heist crew. Mochi was created before Bobo and is one of the oldest characters.

## Vehicles and music

- Bobo's signature car is a **1995 Honda Civic Del Sol**. The real-world Del Sol design has no rear seats.
- Bobo prefers cars that start with a key.
- The Bobo Reborn Del Sol is discovered rusty and non-running in the forest; its key is in the glovebox.
- The restoration is a meaningful long-running project, not an instant fix.
- Koda starts out without a car. Bobo later gives Koda a Toyota/AE86-related car.
- Eurobeat is important to the saga. “Killing My Love” is emotionally significant to Bobo.
- Bobo cares about his speakers and avoids simply blasting them at maximum volume.

## Character portrayal notes

- **Aaron** is a fun-loving monkey. His aunt says he can drive, but manual/stick shift is a complication.
- **Twixxel_lesgo** speaks with a light, readable German-inspired “Vit Style” now and then: “ze,” “zat,” “zis,” “vit,” “zey,” “vere,” “vat,” “tink,” “ting,” “zen,” and “zere.” Keep the voice soft and cute; don't force the accent into every sentence.
- The cast should not curse constantly.
- Keep Minecraft skins distinct from assumptions about a person's real-world identity.
- **SlipperyHC** should not be labeled a furry.
- **Eisuke** is a normal human, not Eisuke_Fur and not a fursuit character.

## Continuity rules

- Use the name **Bobo**, never “Popo” for the main character.
- Don't rewrite a confirmed relationship or character origin without an explicit canon change.
- When a detail is not established, mark it as **open** rather than silently deciding it.
- Comedy and sincere emotional scenes can coexist.
