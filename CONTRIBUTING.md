# Contributing to Bobo Reborn

This repository preserves the user's Bobo Saga canon.

## Before adding a story
1. Check [CANON.md](CANON.md), the character guide, and the vehicle registry.
2. Preserve established relationships and origin details.
3. Keep the jungle as the central setting.
4. If a detail is uncertain, leave it open or ask before treating it as canon.

## Writing chapters
- Use Markdown files in `stories/chapters/`.
- Give each chapter a numbered, descriptive filename.
- Preserve character voices and the balance between comedy and sincere moments.
- Keep recurring projects, especially the Del Sol restoration, consistent across chapters.

## Changing canon
When a new fact becomes permanent, update the relevant guide and add a timeline entry if it affects chronology. Don't erase an established fact just to make a new gag easier.
