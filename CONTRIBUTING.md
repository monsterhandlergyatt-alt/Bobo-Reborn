# Contributing to Bobo Reborn

1. Check [CANON.md](CANON.md) and relevant character/vehicle guides.
2. Preserve established relationships, origins, and vehicle details.
3. Keep the jungle central.
4. If uncertain, mark details open rather than guessing.
5. Add chapters under `stories/chapters/` with numbered filenames.
6. When a fact becomes permanent, update the relevant guide and timeline.
7. Portraits under `assets/characters/` are original stylized SVGs; refine them if reference images become available.
