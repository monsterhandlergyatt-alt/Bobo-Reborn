# Vehicle Registry

## Bobo's 1995 Honda Civic Del Sol
- **Owner/driver:** Bobo
- **Model year:** 1995
- **Condition at rediscovery:** Rusty and non-running
- **Discovery detail:** The key is in the glovebox
- **Story role:** An emotional reunion followed by a garage restoration
- **Design note:** A Honda Civic Del Sol has no rear seats
- **Preference:** Bobo likes key-start cars

### Restoration arc
1. Bobo discovers the abandoned Del Sol in the forest.
2. The discovery lands emotionally; the car represents more than transportation.
3. The car is brought to the tree and then home with Koda.
4. Bobo commits to a garage restoration and buys expensive parts.
5. The restoration is eventually completed.

Treat this as an arc with meaningful steps, not a single scene that skips all the work.
