# Bobo Reborn — Character Roster

This roster records confirmed details. Visual references do not automatically establish a character's full design or personality.

## Core cast

### Bobo
- Main character; spell the name **Bobo**, not Popo.
- Lives in the jungle with Koda.
- Signature car: a 1995 Honda Civic Del Sol with no rear seats.
- Loves cars and eurobeat, prefers key-start cars, and cares about his speakers.
- Finding and restoring the rusty, non-running Del Sol is an emotional milestone.

### Koda
- Bobo's companion/brother and housemate.
- Koda loves Bobo.
- Starts without a car; Bobo later gives him a Toyota/AE86-related car. Exact specification remains open.

### Lila
- Bobo's girlfriend.
- Has a Del Sol; other details remain open unless established in a story.

### Aaron
- Fun-loving monkey. Manual/stick shift can complicate driving.

### Mochi
- Cat hacker associated with the older heist crew.
- Predates Bobo and is one of the saga's oldest characters.

## Continuity rules
- Comedy and sincere moments can coexist; don't make the cast swear constantly.
- Keep the jungle central instead of endlessly expanding into unrelated regions.
- A one-off joke does not automatically become canon.
- When a fact is unknown, mark it as open instead of guessing.
