# Character Guide

## Bobo
The main character of the Bobo Saga. He lives in the jungle with Koda. His 1995 Honda Civic Del Sol is deeply meaningful to him; finding it again and restoring it is one of the emotional pillars of Bobo Reborn.

## Koda
Bobo's companion/brother and housemate. Koda loves Bobo. He initially has no car, but Bobo later gives him a Toyota/AE86-related car.

## Lila
Bobo's girlfriend. She has a Del Sol and is connected to the saga's music-and-car stories.

## Aaron
A fun-loving monkey. His aunt believes he can drive, though driving a manual/stick-shift car may be a problem.

## Mochi
A cat and hacker from the older heist crew. Mochi predates Bobo and is one of the earliest characters in the universe.

## Twixxel_lesgo
Has a soft, cute voice and a light German-inspired “Vit Style.” Use substitutions naturally and sparingly enough that dialogue remains readable.

## ThatMob
Part of the Bobo Reborn cast. Add more profile details when confirmed.

## Eisuke
A normal human character. Do not conflate him with Eisuke_Fur or portray him as wearing a fursuit.

## SlipperyHC
