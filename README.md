# Bobo Reborn 🐒🌴

**The official home for the Bobo Saga's Bobo Reborn era.**

Bobo Reborn follows Bobo and his friends through jungle life, friendship, ridiculous garage disasters, and the emotional return of Bobo's long-lost 1995 Honda Civic Del Sol.

## What is Bobo Reborn?
Bobo Reborn is the current era of the Bobo Saga. Its heart is the jungle and the life Bobo shares with Koda. The story can get chaotic and silly, but it also makes room for sincere moments—especially when Bobo reconnects with his rusty, non-running Del Sol and begins restoring it.

## Canon principles
1. **Bobo, not Popo.**
2. Keep the jungle central; don't expand the setting without a story reason.
3. Preserve established character relationships and vehicle details.
4. Comedy can be chaotic without making every character curse constantly.
5. Treat new ideas as proposals until they are confirmed as canon.
