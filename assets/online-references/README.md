# Online Image Gallery & References

These are **real photos hosted online**, used as visual references for the Bobo Reborn characters and world. They are not photos of the fictional characters themselves, and they do not replace canon or the original stylized portraits. The images are hotlinked from their source pages; they are not stored in this repository. Please check each source's license and terms before reusing an image outside this reference gallery.
