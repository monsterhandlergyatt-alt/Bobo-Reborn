# Online Image Gallery & References

This gallery links to online reference photos and **fan art made by real artists**. Fan art is unofficial and may show artists' personal interpretations rather than the creators' canonical designs. Images remain hosted by their original platforms; check the artist's page for credit, permission, and reuse terms before copying any artwork into the repository.
