# Timeline

Exact dates for individual fictional events remain open unless established.

## Before Bobo Reborn
- Mochi, the cat hacker, predates Bobo and is one of the oldest characters.
- The older heist crew is part of the saga's history.

## Saga begins
- The Bobo Saga began around late 2024 or early 2025.

## Bobo Reborn
- The story returns to a jungle-centered focus.
- Bobo and Koda share a home.
- Bobo finds his rusty, non-running 1995 Honda Civic Del Sol in the forest, with the key in the glovebox.
- The car is brought to familiar places and restored in the garage.
- Koda eventually receives a Toyota/AE86-related car from Bobo.
