# Timeline

This is a broad chronology. Exact dates for individual fictional events remain open unless explicitly established.

## Before Bobo Reborn
- Mochi, the cat hacker, already exists as one of the saga's oldest characters.
- The older heist crew is part of the saga's history.

## Bobo Saga begins
- The saga begins around late 2024 or early 2025.

## Bobo Reborn
- The story returns to a jungle-centered focus.
- Bobo and Koda share a home in the jungle.
- Bobo rediscovers his rusty, non-running 1995 Honda Civic Del Sol in the forest, with the key in the glovebox.
- The Del Sol is brought back to familiar places and restored in a garage.
- Koda eventually receives a Toyota/AE86-related car from Bobo.
